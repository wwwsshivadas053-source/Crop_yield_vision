🌾 Better Crop Planning

Farmer Knowledge Center

💧 Importance of Proper Irrigation

Efficient irrigation helps maintain soil moisture and supports healthy crop growth. Farmers should water crops early morning or evening to reduce water evaporation.

🌿 Fertilizer Management

Balanced fertilizer use improves soil fertility and helps crops grow stronger. Organic compost can also improve long-term soil health.

🐛 Pest Monitoring

Regular crop inspection allows farmers to detect pests and diseases early, preventing major damage to crops.

🌾 Sustainable Farming

Using sustainable farming practices such as crop rotation and organic fertilizers can protect soil fertility and increase long-term productivity.
